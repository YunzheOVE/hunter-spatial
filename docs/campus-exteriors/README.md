# Hunter College 68th Street campus exteriors

Two photo-based AI reconstructions for each of the five buildings in the Hunter Spatial map. These are tour illustrations. The references establish materials and major architectural features. Window counts, entrance details, neighboring buildings, and street furniture can vary in the reconstructions. Google Maps reached the intersection but did not display the Street View panorama in the browser used for research.
